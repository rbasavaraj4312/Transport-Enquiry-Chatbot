# 🚌 Transport-Enquiry-Chatbot 
A centralized, AI-powered platform designed to make bus travel **simpler, smarter, and more efficient**.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔹 Live Bus Tracking  
Get **real-time updates** on bus location so passengers always know where their bus is.

### 🔹 AI Chatbot (RAG + Multi-Agent System)  
Automates bus inquiries, provides route suggestions, handles booking support, and reduces manual workload.

### 🔹 Smart Seat Booking & Payments  
Seamless seat reservation with integrated and secure payment options.

### 🔹 Driver & Passenger Interfaces  
Ensures **transparent communication** between drivers, passengers, and admins at every stage.

---

## 💡 Inspiration  
While traveling, I often found myself waiting at bus stops with **no idea where the bus was**. I’d ask the reception or check manually—an inconvenient and frustrating experience.  
This inspired me to build a **centralized platform** like *“Where Is My Bus”* so passengers can track buses, plan trips efficiently, and avoid unnecessary waiting.

---

## 🌍 Impact for Users

### ⏳ No More Blind Waiting  
Real-time tracking saves precious time and enhances travel reliability.

### 🙌 Reduced Manual Work  
The AI chatbot handles queries, bookings, and assistance instantly.

### 🚌 Centralized Travel Hub  
One app for buses, trains, cabs, and metro services—with comparison features.

### 🌄 Tourism Made Easy  
Example: On a *Ballari → Chikmagalur* trip, the system suggests:  
- Available buses  
- Local attractions  
- Hotels & restaurants  
- Travel agency packages  

### 🤝 Transparency & Trust  
Clear details on routes, seats, and pricing to reduce confusion.

### 🌱 Scalable & Sustainable  
Eco-friendly, efficient, and easily expandable into other transport systems.

---

## 🚊 Future Vision  
To build a **multi-modal, AI-driven travel assistant** that integrates all public transport and tourism services into a single ecosystem—from booking a seat to exploring an entire city.
